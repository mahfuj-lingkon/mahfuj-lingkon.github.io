Key Considerations for Banking IT General Controls

Aug 1, 2025 · 4 min read

IT General Controls (ITGC) form the foundation of a bank's technology control environment. Evaluating these controls requires understanding both the technical landscape and the regulatory context in which banks operate.

Access Management

In banking environments, access management is critical. Auditors should evaluate how user access is provisioned, modified, and deprovisioned. Key areas include privileged access management, role-based access controls, and periodic access reviews. The principle of least privilege should be consistently applied across all systems.

Change Management

Banks must maintain rigorous change management processes to ensure system stability and security. Auditors should assess whether changes are properly authorized, tested, and documented. Emergency change procedures should also be evaluated for appropriate controls and post-implementation review.

IT Operations

Operational controls ensure that IT systems run smoothly and securely. This includes evaluating backup and recovery procedures, incident management processes, and capacity planning. Banks must demonstrate that they can maintain service continuity even during disruptions.

Regulatory Alignment

Bangladesh Bank guidelines and international frameworks like Basel provide the regulatory context for banking ITGC. Auditors must ensure that control assessments align with these requirements and that findings are communicated in a way that supports regulatory compliance.
