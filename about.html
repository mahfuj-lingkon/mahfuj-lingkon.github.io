About Me

Information Systems Auditor | Technology Risk, Cybersecurity & AI

Professional Profile

I am an Information Systems Auditor with 4+ years of experience across banking operations, cybersecurity, and IT governance. Currently serving as Senior Officer (IS Audit) at City Bank PLC, Dhaka, I help organizations strengthen their technology control environments and reduce risk exposure.

My career spans IS audit, cybersecurity engineering, and technology risk management. I have delivered audit services to leading banking and financial institutions, conducted vulnerability assessments and penetration tests, and implemented security automation solutions.

I hold the CISA, CEH, and ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certifications, and I am passionate about leveraging AI and automation to enhance audit efficiency and security operations.

My approach combines technical depth with business understanding — ensuring that technology controls not only meet compliance requirements but also support organizational objectives.

Quick Facts

  • Certifications: CISA, CEH, ISO 27001 LA
  • Education: B.Sc. Software Engineering (Cybersecurity)
  • Interests: AI in audit, security automation, threat intelligence
