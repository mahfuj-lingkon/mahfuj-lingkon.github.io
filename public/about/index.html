About Me

Cybersecurity Engineer & IS Auditor | Technology Risk, Cybersecurity & AI

Professional Profile

I am a Cybersecurity Engineer and IT Auditor with 4+ years of hands-on experience across banking security, SOC/SIEM, penetration testing, malware analysis, and GRC. Currently serving as Senior Officer (IS Audit) at City Bank PLC, Dhaka, I help organizations strengthen their technology control environments and reduce risk exposure.

My career spans IS audit, cybersecurity engineering, and technology risk management across City Bank PLC, Eastern Bank PLC, ITCOM, and ACNABIN. I have delivered audit services to leading banking and financial institutions, conducted malware investigations and threat intelligence analysis, and managed Fasoo encryption systems.

I hold the CISA, CEH, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor, and multiple TCM Security certifications. I am passionate about leveraging AI and automation to enhance audit efficiency and security operations.

My approach combines technical depth with business understanding — ensuring that technology controls not only meet compliance requirements but also support organizational objectives.

Quick Facts

  • Certifications: CISA, CEH, ISO 27001 LA, TCM Security
  • Education: B.Sc. Software Engineering (Cybersecurity)
