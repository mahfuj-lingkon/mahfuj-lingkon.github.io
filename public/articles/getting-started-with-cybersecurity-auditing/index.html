Getting Started with Cybersecurity Auditing

Jun 20, 2025 · 1 min read

As cyber threats continue to evolve, the demand for cybersecurity auditors is growing rapidly. For traditional IS auditors looking to expand into this domain, here are the essential knowledge areas and skills to develop.

Understand the Threat Landscape

Cybersecurity auditing requires a solid understanding of the threat landscape. This includes knowledge of common attack vectors, threat actors, and the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) they use. Frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK provide a structured way to understand adversary behavior.

Learn Security Frameworks

Familiarize yourself with key cybersecurity frameworks and standards. ISO 27001, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and CIS Controls provide structured approaches to assessing cybersecurity posture. Understanding these frameworks allows auditors to evaluate controls against recognized benchmarks.

Develop Technical Foundations

While cybersecurity auditors don’t need to be penetration testers, a solid technical foundation is essential. This includes understanding network security, encryption, identity and access management, and security architecture. Certifications like CEH can help build this knowledge.

Bridge Audit and Security

The most effective cybersecurity auditors can bridge the gap between traditional audit methodology and security expertise. This means applying risk-based audit approaches to security controls, communicating findings in business terms, and providing actionable recommendations that improve security posture.
